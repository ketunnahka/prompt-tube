Privacy Policy for Youtube Recommendation Fixer

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Youtube Recommendation Fixer ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices.

1. Data Collection and Usage
The Extension operates entirely locally within your browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, analytics, or search queries with any external servers. 

2. Justification of Permissions
- Host Permissions (://.youtube.com/): Used strictly locally to read and modify the YouTube interface (hiding native recommendations and injecting custom video cards) and to fetch raw search results directly from YouTube.
- Scripting: Used to inject the custom UI elements into the YouTube page.
- Storage: Used to save your custom search prompts locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services
The Extension communicates directly with YouTube to fetch search results. We do not use any external APIs, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

4. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
